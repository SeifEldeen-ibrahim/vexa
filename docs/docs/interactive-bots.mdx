<Warning>
**Honest status:** the interactive family is **partly available**. In-meeting **chat — read and
write — works on Google Meet**, and the assistant can answer questions asked in the meeting chat.
Speak is proven at the bot layer but still unreachable through the API; screen share and avatar are
contract-sealed and unimplemented. The API shape for the remaining surfaces is still under design —
it is **not** a straight restoration of the pre-0.12 endpoints.
</Warning>

<Note>
Every interactive command is gated by **`VOICE_AGENT_ENABLED`**, off by default. A bot that can type
into and speak in your meeting is opt-in per deployment and per request — send it with
`"voice_agent_enabled": true` on `POST /bots`, or set `VOICE_AGENT_ENABLED=true` for the deployment.
A bot sent without it ignores every interactive command and says so in its logs.
</Note>

## Per-surface status (0.12, verified against the tree)

| Surface | Contract (`api.v1`) | Reachable today | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat **capture** | — | ✅ in the transcript | messages typed in the meeting arrive as transcript segments (Google Meet, Jitsi) |
| Chat **write** | sealed | ✅ `POST /bots/{platform}/{native_meeting_id}/chat` | Google Meet; needs `VOICE_AGENT_ENABLED`. Returns `202` — the command is published to the bot, not confirmed delivered |
| Chat **read** | sealed | ✅ `GET /bots/{platform}/{native_meeting_id}/chat` | mounted and served, but always an **empty list** — the durable record of a meeting's chat is its transcript |
| **Speak** | sealed | ❌ 404 | proven live at the bot layer (acts.v1 `speak` → TTS → virtual mic), not wired at the API layer — tracked in [#514](https://github.com/Vexa-ai/vexa/issues/514) |
| Screen share | sealed | ❌ 404 | planned back in 0.12.x |
| Avatar / virtual camera | sealed | ❌ 404 | planned back in 0.12.x |

"Sealed" means the endpoint shape is frozen in the public API contract; it does not mean served. A call
to an unmounted surface returns `404` on every deployment.

## What you can do today

- **Send a message into a live meeting** (Google Meet):

  ```bash
  curl -X POST https://api.cloud.vexa.ai/bots/google_meet/abc-defg-hij/chat \
    -H "X-API-Key: $VEXA_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"text": "Sharing the notes doc after this call."}'
  ```

  `202` means the command reached the bot, not that the message was typed — the bot may have left,
  or been sent without `VOICE_AGENT_ENABLED`. Its logs say which. Messages are capped at 500
  characters, Google Meet's own composer limit.

- **Read what was typed in the meeting** from the transcript: chat messages appear as ordinary
  transcript segments, attributed to their sender, alongside speech.

- **Let the assistant answer in the meeting chat.** With `VEXA_MEET_CHAT_ENABLED=true`, a participant
  typing `@vexa <question>` gets an answer in that chat, grounded in the live transcript. The
  exchange appears in the Terminal's Assistant tab as a normal conversation thread, so anyone can
  carry it on there. `VEXA_MEET_CHAT_PREFIX` changes the address.

- **Capture and transcribe** as usual — the interactive gaps do not affect transcription, recordings,
  or the transcript API.

<Warning>
**Anyone in the meeting can address the assistant, and it answers as you.** The `@vexa` prefix
decides whether a line is *meant* for the bot; it is not a permission check, and a guest in the room
can type it. The turn runs under the meeting owner's identity — so whatever it can read, it can read
**aloud into a room you do not control**.

That is why the default is narrow: a meeting-chat turn is grounded in **that meeting's transcript
only**, and is dispatched with **no tools**, so it cannot open a file even in a workspace that is
mounted. There is nothing private in scope to leak.

Read-only mounts alone would *not* be enough here. They stop a participant changing your workspace;
they do nothing about one asking the assistant to read your notes out to the room.
</Warning>

### Letting it see more than the meeting

On a live meeting the Terminal shows a toggle: **`@vexa: transcript only`** ⇄ **`@vexa: workspace`**.

Granting `workspace` lets that meeting's assistant also read your workspace, read-only — which
includes **past meetings' notes**, since the copilot writes those regardless of this setting. Grant
it when the room is people you trust with that material, and remember that everyone in the room can
then ask for it.

The grant is **per meeting**, expires after 12 hours, and is off again for the next meeting — a room
you trusted last week is not the room you are in today. Anything that goes wrong reading the grant
(store unreachable, unknown value) falls back to transcript-only.

## Where this is going

The pre-0.12 shape was REST-per-surface (`POST /bots/.../speak`, `/chat`, `/screen`). That design predates
the 0.12 agent layer, and the open question is **where interactive capabilities belong** — bot-level REST,
or the Agent API driving the bot's proven acts layer (cf. the external request to
[attach an external agent by URL, #333](https://github.com/Vexa-ai/vexa/issues/333)). That design decision
is tracked openly; demand for this family is measured and sustained, and comments on the design issue are
exactly the evidence the decision consumes.
